About

Who is writing all this

I was born in Ukraine and now live in the UK. I write in two languages and think in a third — the one where the cats talk back.

My first English-language book, Pegasus for Hire, is a collection of urban fantasy short stories: the kind where the magic has a day job and the day job has opinions. English is not my first language, which I consider an advantage — I still notice how strange it is.

Before that I wrote the seventeenth and final volume of the Russian fantasy series Aqua. North–South, closing a story that sixteen other authors had started. And in an entirely different life I wrote a non-fiction book about the Siamese cat, credited on the cover as a national-category cat expert. Both things are true and both still surprise me.

I also draw. The illustrations on this site are mine, which means every mistake in them is mine too.
